**Planned change (as of 2026-04-18, not yet implemented):**

Restructure rental income collection so that all rent (Park View, Hillside) flows into the **ANZ One Offset Account**, which in turn feeds mortgage repayments for all three loans (Sassafras PPOR, Park View investment, Hillside investment).

**Why:** Maximises offset balance → reduces interest on the PPOR loan (non-deductible interest is the expensive kind). Consolidates rental income into one predictable stream for easier monitoring and cash-flow forecasting. Eliminates the current fragmentation where rent lands in CBA, Virgin Go, and ANZ across different timing.

**How to apply:**
- Until restructure is live, expect rental income to arrive in multiple accounts with mixed payee strings — don't assume clean attribution.
- Once live, Rentals category should only show credits into ANZ Offset; anything elsewhere is a flag for investigation.
- Mortgage repayments should show as clean transfers from ANZ Offset → each of the three loan accounts.
- Flag to Scott when reviewing April/May data: compare before-and-after to confirm the new architecture is working as intended.
