Scott and Mina jointly own three Victorian properties:

1. **31 Sassafras Drive, Sunbury VIC** — current PPOR. In **Jacksons Hill** estate (premium Sunbury pocket, 3-min drive from town centre, walking distance to train/cafes/parks, larger land). ~808 sqm block. Settled Oct 2025, purchase price $830K (negotiated down from $870K ask on back of building inspection), current est. value ~$900K (market dip since purchase). Purchased using equity extracted from Hillside.
2. **18 Park View Terrace, Sydenham VIC** — investment property, currently leased arms-length via property manager. Previously the PPOR before Sassafras. Current est. value ~$650K. Gross rent **$2,172/mo**, net of management fees ~**$2,040/mo**.
3. **3 Celendine Place, Hillside VIC** — effectively the "family home" / safe haven. Mina's family lives there and pays partial rent **$2,200/mo** (below-market) that helps service the loan. Originally the first residence (loan is the oldest); equity was extracted to buy Sassafras. Not a pure arms-length investment.

**Expected total monthly rental income: ~$4,240** (Park View net + Hillside).

**ANZ loan mapping (as of 2026-04-18):**

Product labels are inverted vs actual use — PPOR sits on an investment-loan product, Hillside (investment) sits on a home-loan product. Likely a legacy of the Hillside→Sassafras equity extraction. Interest deductibility follows purpose of borrowing, not the product label, but worth accountant eyeball once.

**Why this matters:**

- Hillside is financially an investment property (generates rental income, loan interest likely deductible by purpose) but emotionally/strategically a family asset — don't suggest selling it or optimising it purely on yield.
- The Hillside → Sassafras equity linkage means the three properties are financially interdependent; decisions on one affect borrowing capacity on others.
- Partial rent from Mina's family means rental income modelling can't assume market rates.

**How to apply:** When assessing wealth, debt ratios, or restructure options, treat Sydenham as the only pure-investment property. Flag Hillside's dual role in any scenario modelling. Don't recommend changes to Hillside without explicitly acknowledging the family-safe-haven constraint. If asked to restructure loans, flag the product/purpose mismatch for accountant review.
