# Evidence bundle — adversarial check on the value-sim CONCLUSIONS

Same athlete as before (38M, basketball, time-capped ~4-5 slots/wk, post-illness re-entry ramp, DB/KB only).

## What the simulation did
Modelled each session as a SATURATING coverage problem over 13 goal sub-dimensions
(Longevity x3, Aesthetics x4, Basketball x6), equal goal weights. A 2nd movement
hitting an already-covered sub-dimension adds ~0 ("don't double-count"). Ranked
movements by marginal value/min and by UNIQUE coverage (value lost if removed entirely).

## Findings I intend to act on
1. **Backbone confirmed, robust across saturation params:** highest value/min = broad
   compounds (Carries, DB Row, Incline press, Chin-up/Pull-up, Jumps, 4x4). LPP shape stands.
2. **4-day base captures 77% of max cross-domain value; full 7-day only 86%.** The 3 bonus
   days add ~9 pts → quantitatively backs "the 4-day base IS the program."
3. **Base's weakest goal is Aesthetics (69% vs L 91% / B 72%)** because pull + upper-hypertrophy
   volume is bonus-gated — and aesthetics (leanness/V-taper) is the athlete's TOP stated lever.
4. **The two highest value/min movements (Carries #1, DB Row #2) currently sit on the
   always-skipped bonus Thursday.** PROPOSED FIX: fold DB Row + a Carry onto the protected
   Tuesday upper day (Push -> "Upper": push + pull + carry), so the single best movements
   happen even when Thu is skipped, and the base aesthetics gap (69%) closes.
5. **Armor (soleus/calf, Copenhagen, Pallof) has near-zero UNIQUE coverage (0.01-0.04)** —
   its sub-dims are covered by RDL/row/carry/decel anyway. PROPOSED: keep it ONLY as a small
   injury-insurance micro-dose woven into protected Monday — never its own time block. (Its
   value is downside/injury-probability reduction, which the coverage model does not price.)
6. **Skills = highest unique coverage outside conditioning (3.97)** — sole supplier of the
   SKILL sub-dimension. Low on breadth-math, irreplaceable on coverage. Keep on court/game/Sat.
