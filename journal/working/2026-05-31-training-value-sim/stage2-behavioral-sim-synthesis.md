## Convergent findings (binding signal)
1. **[5/5] Aesthetics — the TOP goal (40%) — is UNDER-served, because its accelerator volume (Fri pump) sits on the lowest-adherence day.** Outcome scores 3–3.5. Opus: "built like a longevity plan wearing an aesthetics label." **Same stranded-value pathology as v6.2/v6.3 — best work on the skipped day.**
2. **[4/5] #1 FIX converges: load the V-taper / pull / aesthetic volume onto the FIXED days; make Fri a tiny optional pump, not the carrier.** (Opus: make Tue a true Upper push/pull day. Sonnet: move the pump to Thursday — fresh, 48h post-game, actually gets done. Codex: move Fri's real work to Tue/Thu, Fri = laterals/curls/light rows only, no pressing to failure.)
3. **[4/5] Monday is over-stuffed** — jumps + sprints + decel + leg strength + armor + mobility in one session. The CNS-consolidation over-corrected; last items degrade → injury. Calf/Achilles strain ~wk 3–5 from the Monday load + the "first good week" overreach (Opus, Sonnet, Haiku, Gemini). Gemini's fix: move accel sprints OFF Monday.
4. **[3/5] Upper-press redundancy** — Tue press-to-failure + Fri pump both hit incline/dips/lateral → shoulder/elbow irritation AND a push>pull imbalance that works against the V-taper (Opus, Sonnet, Codex).
5. **[3-4/5] Conditioning (4×4) + mobility under-secured** — both live on flaky reward days; Gemini scored Longevity 2 / Mobility 2 ("chronic cardio deficiency"; micro-dose mobility ≠ ROM change). Opus dissented (called longevity over-served).

## Weighted verdict (convergent)
The week **inverts the 40/20/20/20 weighting in practice**: Basketball + lower-power (lower-weighted, on sticky fixed days) are well-served; Aesthetics (top) is under-served; upper PULL (back/rear-delt = the V-taper) and reliable hypertrophy frequency are the biggest gaps.

## Binding revision → v7.1
- The **3 fixed days must fully deliver the aesthetic (push AND pull) volume on their own.** Fri demoted to a genuinely optional small pump (laterals/curls/light rows — no failure pressing).
- **Move accel sprints off Monday** (to the fresh Sat engine+court day, giving Saturday its own reason to happen) → de-risks the calf and unclutters Monday.
- **Balance push/pull** across the fixed days; stop double-pressing Tue+Fri.
- **Secure conditioning** by pairing 4×4 + sprints + court into one identity-day (Sat "athlete day") rather than a flaky add-on.
