## Systematic bias
- L over-credits generic resistance work as 4; should distinguish high-return strength patterns from accessory.
- B under-credits strength transfer for contact/post-up (rows, split squats, hinges, carries).
- A over-credits muscle-building that doesn't affect the stated top lever (leanness/chafe).
- Lower-leg/adductor/prehab under-valued for B/L given injury-resilience objective.

## High-variance
- 4×4 (recovery-gated), sprints (B=5 only post-ramp), plyo (low-contact/good-landing only), BSS (strength-bias not size), skills (live-constraint vs casual), med-ball (explosive intent).
