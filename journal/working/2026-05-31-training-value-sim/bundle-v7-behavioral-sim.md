# Virtual-athlete 12-week simulation — v7-draft week

You are simulating a real athlete living this exact weekly program for 12 weeks. Be brutally
realistic about what a busy 38-year-old actually does, not what's optimal on paper.

## The athlete
38M, CEO, father of four, time-poor. Recreational basketball. Goals weighted **Aesthetics 40 /
Longevity 20 / Basketball 20 / Mobility 20** — leanness + V-taper is his TOP lever. Currently
~1 month post a respiratory infection + a fasting block (deconditioned, re-entry ramp) — but
simulate the STEADY-STATE week he ramps into, not the ramp itself. Equipment: adjustable DBs to
21 kg, KBs to 24 kg, flat+incline bench, plyo box, bike, treadmill, HR strap, band. No barbell/
machines/cables. History (real): he skips midweek "extra" days under fatigue; the guilt of
missing planned days is itself a quit-risk; he tends to overreach the first week he feels good
and tweak something (calf/shoulder/elbow).

## The v7-draft week (steady-state target)
Structure = 3 fixed strength days (his LPP) + 3 zero-guilt "reward" days + basketball.
Sequencing intent: ALL high-CNS lower-body work (jumps, sprints, leg strength) is consolidated
onto Monday; the game (also high-CNS lower) is 48 h later on Wednesday; nothing else high-CNS-
lower sits adjacent. Aesthetics volume (his top goal) is loaded so the FIXED days carry it, with
the reward days accelerating it.

| Day | Session | Tier | Contents |
|-----|---------|------|----------|
| **Mon** | LOWER + POWER | FIXED | t-spine/hip mobility · jumps · accel sprints · ATG split squat or Bulgarian (strength, 2–3 RIR, explosive, NOT to failure) · single-leg RDL · armor (soleus, Copenhagen, Pallof) · decel ladder |
| **Tue** | PUSH | FIXED | Incline DB press (to failure) · Dips · Lateral raise · OHP · triceps |
| **Wed** | GAME | basketball | full-court basketball (conditioning + skill + contact + COD) |
| **Thu** | PULL | FIXED | Chin-ups/Pull-ups · DB Row · loaded carry · face pull · curls |
| **Fri** | UPPER PUMP | reward | extra push+pull hypertrophy volume (incline, lateral, row, dips, curls) — the "aesthetic accelerator" |
| **Sat** | CONDITIONING + COURT | reward | 4×4 intervals · supplementary skills (shooting, post-up, light COD) |
| **Sun** | rest / optional Zone-2 | reward | — |

Set scheme: upper aesthetics = 3 sets, last to failure, double progression. Lower = 3 sets,
3–5 reps, 2–3 RIR, explosive (lean/power, not hypertrophy). Armor = small non-negotiable
micro-dose. Skill = piggybacked on game + court + daily home dribbling.
