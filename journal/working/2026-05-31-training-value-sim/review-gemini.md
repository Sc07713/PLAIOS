# Gemini review — L/A/B score validation (2026-05-31)

## Re-calibrations (≥1 pt)
- 4×4 conditioning **A 4→2** — HIIT is a calorie burner not a V-taper tool; cardio interference can blunt A.
- DB Row **B 2→4** — rebounding/boxing-out/post-up rely on horizontal pulling + scapular retraction.
- Acceleration sprints **L 4→2** — high CNS/tissue tax + injury risk for deconditioned 38M; marginal longevity vs 4×4/Z2.
- Jumps/plyo **L 3→1** — in re-entry ramp, Achilles/tendon rupture risk outweighs BMD benefit.
- Decel ladder **L 3→1** — sport-specific; zero all-cause-mortality carryover.
- Incline DB Press **L 4→2** — upper-chest hypertrophy is aesthetic, not a longevity pillar.
- Skills **L 1→2** — cognitive load / reactive coordination = emerging brain-health longevity lever [<85%].

## Systematic bias
- **L-inflation:** Longevity used as proxy for "general movement" not specific mortality levers (VO2max, grip, leg power, stability).
- **A-inflation for cardio:** over-crediting conditioning for aesthetics; leanness is ~90% kitchen — reserve A for silhouette-changing hypertrophy.

## High variance
- Sprints/plyo: 0 or 5 depending on whether re-entry hurdles cleared (game-changer vs season-ender).
- 4×4: if its fatigue causes a missed resistance session, net A/B becomes negative.

## Missing movements
- **Face pulls** L4/A4/B3 (3.67) — shoulder health (L), rear-delt/trap width (A), upright posture for contact (B).
- **Chin-ups (neutral)** L4/A5/B3 (4.00) — likely outscores DB Row; better bicep recruitment for A while keeping back health.

## Constraint note
- BSS / SL-RDL: with 21 kg DB ceiling, the L-score (strength/sarcopenia) decays within ~3 months once loading ceiling is hit.
