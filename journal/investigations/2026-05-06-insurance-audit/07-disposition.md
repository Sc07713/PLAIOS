## The binding decision

The audit closes with the following binding outcome for the in-scope general-insurance lines:

### 1. CBA Insurance / Hollard — DROPPED across all four lines

The CBA quotes Mina pulled are not a viable switch. Hollard underwrites them and is the subject of active ASIC litigation (25-057MR) for severe claims-handling failures on a regional Victorian home claim — the exact failure mode (18-month delay, refused make-safe, property uninhabitable) that an under-buffered household with $4.5k liquid cannot tolerate on its PPOR. The premium savings (~$1,691/yr) do not compensate for this tail risk.

This applies across the bundle, not just PPOR — operational simplicity (one carve-out, not four) and the expectation that AAMI / Budget Direct / QBE will price competitively on car make selective inclusion of CBA-on-car not worth the cognitive load.

### 2. Comparator shortlist (final) — quote in this order

**For the 3-property bundle (PPOR + 2 landlord):**
- Budget Direct (re-quote fresh as new business — current insurer, Canstar 2026 winner)
- AAMI (Finder 2026 Best Value Home; Suncorp Group)
- RACV (VIC local; Outstanding Customer Satisfaction 3 years; pattern-match for high-friction family claims)
- QBE (Canstar 2026 home & contents winner; landlord product depth; quality-tier anchor)

**For car (quote standalone, do not bundle with home):**
- Budget Direct (Finder 2026 Best Value Car)
- AAMI
- Bingle (Auto & General's price-aggressive sub-brand)

### 3. Hillside — pivot product type

Stop treating Hillside as a "landlord insurance" search. Mina's mother + siblings are family tenants; standard Australian landlord policies will not pay rent default or tenant damage for related parties even if the policy is sold.

Replace with: **building + public liability cover (Investment / Owner-not-occupier)**, with the related-party tenancy disclosed at quote stage. **RACV and QBE are reported to write family-let cleanly with disclosure**; Terri Scheer generally declines related-party tenancy (so Terri Scheer can be quoted for Sydenham but not Hillside).

Disclose at quote: Hillside is owner-occupier-equivalent in risk profile (a family dwelling) and the insurer is being asked to cover the building structure, fixtures, and landlord public liability — not loss-of-rent.

**Method:** Mina runs the insurer's rebuild calculator on each property when getting quotes. If the calculator suggests higher than the anchor, take the calculator number. Don't manually under-insure to save premium — the contents/building gap cost is far higher than the premium delta.

### 5. Bundle strategy

- **Bundle building + 2 landlord** with one insurer for the combined-policy discount (typically 10–15%).
- **Quote car standalone.** Combined-policy discount on car rarely overcomes Budget Direct / Bingle's car-specific base rate advantage.

### 6. Hillside tenancy — required prep before any quote

Before quoting Hillside or relying on any tenant-related cover at any property:

- Ensure a **written lease** with Mina's mother + siblings.
- Ensure rent is **at market rate** (or near it) and recorded.
- Ensure a **bond** is held (in line with VIC Residential Tenancies Authority practice).
- Disclose related-party tenancy in writing on every quote.
- Get insurer's **written confirmation of acceptance** of the related-party arrangement before binding cover.

### 7. Switch sequencing

- **Re-quote Budget Direct first** as new business. Auto & General's discount-strip ASIC matter (26-036MR) suggests current renewal-book customers may be silently un-discounted. New-business pricing is typically 10–30% below renewal pricing.
- **Then quote AAMI + RACV + QBE** in parallel. About 1.5 hours of Mina's time per insurer for a full bundle.
- **Decide based on filled comparison sheet** at `05-research-pack.md`.

### 8. Acceptance criteria for the final switch decision

- For PPOR: choose lowest premium among insurers without active ASIC claims-handling litigation, where the rebuild sum is verified-adequate.
- For investments: lowest competent premium where cover terms (tenant damage, building, public liability) match.
- For car: lowest premium where excess + listed-driver schedule + cover scope are equivalent.
