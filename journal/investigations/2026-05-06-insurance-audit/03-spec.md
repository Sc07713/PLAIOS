## Decision criteria (in priority order)

1. **Adequacy of cover** — sums insured cover full rebuild + realistic contents replacement; standard exclusions don't bite.
2. **Underwriter financial strength + claims reputation** — APRA financial-strength rating, Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA) complaint rate, Choice/Canstar reputation.
3. **Total annual premium across the bundle** — including any combined-policy discount.
4. **Excess flexibility** — ability to lift excess to lower premium if buffer permits (limited here, $4.5k liquid).
5. **Switching friction** — Budget Direct policies aren't locked in; switch any time. Health/pet not in scope.

## Related-party tenancy note (Hillside)

Tenants at Hillside are Mina's mother + siblings. Most Australian landlord policies handle this as follows:
- Building / contents (landlord-owned) cover: normal
- Loss of rent: usually excluded or reduced when tenant is related (insurer's view: enforcement is unreliable in family lets)
- Tenant damage / theft: usually still covered if there's a formal lease

**Action in research phase:** identify which insurers will write related-party landlord policies and what the specific exclusions/loadings are.
