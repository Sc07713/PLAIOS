Gemini says drop entirely (claims-handling reputation taints the brand at any line). Resolution: dropping CBA entirely is operationally simpler than line-by-line; the small-saving optimisation isn't worth the cognitive load given Budget Direct/AAMI/QBE will be cheaper than CBA on car anyway. **Drop CBA across all lines.**
