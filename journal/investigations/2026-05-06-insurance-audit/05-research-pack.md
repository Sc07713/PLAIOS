## Things to test on each quote

1. **Annual vs monthly billing** — annual usually saves 10–25%.
2. **Combined-policy bundle discount** — typically 10–15% if home + landlord + car all with one insurer. Confirm it applies to all three lines.
3. **Online discount** — most insurers offer ~10% for completing a quote online.
4. **Flood cover** — should be included as standard in 2026 Australian policies; confirm.
5. **Storm / fire / theft inclusions** — standard but confirm.
6. **Excess on water damage / flood / motor burnout** — sometimes higher than the basic excess; check.
7. **Sum-insured rebuild calculator** — use each insurer's calculator for Sassafras and Hillside; flag if they suggest higher than the current $450k / $350k.
8. **AAMI landlord carpet exclusion** — known gotcha; ask explicitly if carpet is covered for tenant damage.
9. **Allianz renovation / short-term-letting exclusion** — only relevant if you might Airbnb either property.

## What to AVOID

- **CBA Insurance / Hollard / Real Insurance / Woolworths Insurance** for home cover — same underwriter (Hollard / HIP), active ASIC litigation for delayed / denied claims. The 18-month claim delay case in regional Victoria is the precedent.
- **Apia** — over-50s only; not eligible.
- **No-name comparator brands** that don't show their underwriter — verify the underwriter is APRA-licensed and not Hollard.

## Decision rule

For each line item, choose the lowest premium **provided** the underwriter has no active claims-handling ASIC matter and the cover terms (sums insured, excess, exclusions) are at least equivalent. If the cheapest is borderline on cover, take the second cheapest.

For your situation specifically:
- **PPOR (Sassafras):** prioritise claims reputation > price. A delayed claim here is catastrophic.
- **Investment properties (Sydenham, Hillside):** price-sensitivity higher; AFCA dispute capacity exists if claims are delayed.
- **Car:** lowest competent premium wins; cars are replaceable.

## Time to run quotes

About 15-20 minutes per insurer per quote tool. Three insurers × 4 lines = ~3 hours of Mina's time, BUT most insurer tools auto-bundle home + landlord + car into a single quote flow, so 1-1.5 hours per insurer is more realistic for a full bundle.
