# Decision — Lean Athlete v6 program review (Codex + Gemini)

## What was decided

The v6.0 training program (basketball overlay on longevity base; lean legs / upper hypertrophy / "earn-it" tiered week) was reviewed adversarially by **Codex (gpt-5.5)** and on external-pattern grounds by **Gemini**. The two models converged independently on the same top issues. Convergence is binding; v6.1 applies the fixes.

## Convergent findings (both models, high confidence)

1. **Power/sprints on Thursday — the morning after Wednesday's game — is a hamstring/Achilles trap.** Gemini: violates High-Low CNS spacing (Charlie Francis). **Both ranked this #1.** → Power moved to a fresh early-week slot (Sun sprints / Mon jumps); Thu became a low/recovery day (pull + core + Zone-2).
2. **Sun 4×4 + heavy legs same day = concurrent-training interference** (Gemini: Baar 2014; Murach & Bagley 2016) — molecular (AMPK↔mTOR) + glycogen/neural. → Decoupled: Sun running, Mon lower lifting.
3. **Lean-legs thesis was half-wrong** — 3×6 *to failure* is a hypertrophy trigger. Real lean lever = heavy low-rep at **2–3 RIR, explosive, not to failure.** → Legs RIR-based/explosive; failure reserved for upper body.
4. **Missing deceleration/landing** (basketball injuries are braking/cutting). → Decel ladder added.
5. **Missing soleus (bent-knee) calf, lateral-plane leg work, eccentric hamstring (Nordic/slider), ankle/foot tissue.** → Added to Mon lower + prehab.
6. **Failure on everything while post-illness + in deficit = recovery tax.** → No failure first 2 wk; criteria-gated ramp; maintenance calories until recovery markers green.
7. **Friday 8-exercise to-failure session unrealistic.** → Trimmed to ~35-min pump.
8. **Base week underdelivers if earned days skip.** → Base-day microdoses (core/prehab/skills).

## Endorsed — kept unchanged

- Unilateral leg bias (spine-sparing, basketball-specific) — Gemini.
- Skills piggybacked on the kids' game venues — Gemini: "elite behavioral design."
- Base/Rush/earn autoregulation — Gemini: maps to Jovanović Agile Periodization / minimum-effective-dose.

## Dissent / open

- Gemini suggested submaximal *all* leg compounds; Codex framed it as "define leg target by measurement not ideology." Resolved: legs RIR-based + track girth/performance, revisit if power stalls.
- Orchestrator (Scott) is tie-breaker on the structural reorder (power off the post-game day changes his preferred "Thu Power & Core" identity). Flagged for his sign-off.
