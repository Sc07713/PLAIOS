**Context:** Mina pulled CBA Insurance quotes for car / Sassafras home / Sydenham landlord, showing ~$1,691/yr saving over current Budget Direct policies. Audit triggered to validate the switch and ensure value for money on the building / contents / car insurance bundle.

**Options Considered:**
- Switch to CBA / Hollard bundle (-$1,691/yr) — **rejected**
- Stay on Budget Direct as-is — superseded by re-quoting strategy
- Build 4-way comparison: Budget Direct (re-quoted) + AAMI + RACV + QBE — **selected**
- Add specialist landlord insurer (Terri Scheer) for Sydenham — **optional**, exclude for Hillside
- Stand-alone car quotes vs bundling — **stand-alone** chosen for car

**Decision:**
1. **Drop CBA / Hollard** from all four lines. Active ASIC litigation (25-057MR) for severe claims-handling failures + 1.3/5 Trustpilot + AFCA criticism is incompatible with a $4.5k-liquid sole-earner household using its PPOR as primary security.
2. **Quote against Budget Direct (re-quoted), AAMI, RACV, QBE** for the property bundle (PPOR + Sydenham landlord + Hillside building).
3. **Quote car standalone** against Budget Direct, AAMI, Bingle.
4. **Pivot Hillside** from landlord product to building + public liability (Owner-not-occupier) cover with related-party tenancy disclosed; Australian landlord rent-default and tenant-damage cover does not apply to family tenants regardless of policy schedule.
5. **Lift sums insured.** Sassafras contents $20k → $120k floor. Building rebuilds to be calculator-verified (likely $600k+ for Sassafras, $500k+ for Sydenham — both currently under-insured).
6. **Bundle the buildings, stand-alone the car** — combined-policy discount on car rarely overcomes price-leader car base rates.
7. **Hillside tenancy prep:** written lease, market rent, bond, written insurer acceptance of related-party arrangement before binding.

**Expected Outcome:**
- Total premium expected to **rise**, not fall — adding Hillside cover + correcting under-insured contents/buildings outweighs any switching saving. Audit deliverable is right-sized cover at the best available price, not a saving for its own sake.
- Switch decision is data-dependent: Mina runs the quotes (~1.5 hours per insurer) using the comparison sheet at `05-research-pack.md` and selects per the decision rules in `07-disposition.md`.
- **Hillside priority:** target binding cover within 4 weeks. Property currently uninsured against $490k of mortgage debt is uncapped exposure.
