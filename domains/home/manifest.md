# Home Domain Manifest

## Always Loaded

These files are loaded into context on every Home domain interaction:

- `claude.md` — Domain identity, hierarchy ownership, decision frameworks, escalation triggers.
- `memory.md` — Persistent facts about property, vehicles, maintenance history, digital systems.
- `state/current-assessment.md` — Latest dimensional assessment of all owned hierarchy levels.

## On Demand

Loaded when relevant to the current task:

- `state/active-priorities.md` — Current focus, queued items, and blocked work.
- `reference/` — Supporting documents, manuals, policy details, vendor contacts.
