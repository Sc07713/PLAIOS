# Mina's Complete Protocol
### Supplements - Diet - Exercise
*Current as of March 2026 -- Review at ferritin retest (8 weeks) and when breastfeeding ceases*

---

## Phase Structure

| Phase | Trigger | Focus |
|-------|---------|-------|
| **Phase 1** | Now -> ferritin >50 ng/mL (~8-12 weeks) | Iron repletion, energy restoration, build exercise habit |
| **Phase 2** | Ferritin >50 ng/mL | Add moderate caloric deficit, increase training intensity |
| **Phase 3** | Post-weaning | Full stack reassessment, accelerate fat loss |

**Do not aggressively restrict calories during Phase 1.** Low intake = low dietary iron at the exact time stores need replenishing. Energy first, fat loss second.

## Supplement Stack

### Morning (with breakfast -- iron rules apply)

| Supplement | Dose | Notes |
|------------|------|-------|
| **Iron** (ferrous bisglycinate) | 60-80mg elemental | **Every other day only** -- daily dosing triggers hepcidin surge, blocking next-day absorption by 35-45% |
| **Vitamin C** | 200mg | Take together with iron |
| **D3/K2** | 4,000 IU D3 / 100mcg K2 | Upgraded from 2,000 IU -- 4,000 IU is the evidence-supported dose for repletion |
| **Omega-3** | 2g EPA/DHA | EPA-predominant formula preferred for mood |
| **Creatine** | 3-5g | Daily -- saturation requires consistent dosing |
| **B12** (methylcobalamin) | 500-1,000mcg | Breastfeeding exports B12 directly into milk; deficiency = fatigue + brain fog |
| **Methylfolate** (5-MTHF) | 400-800mcg | Preferred over folic acid -- 40% of women carry MTHFR variants that block folic acid conversion |
| **Iodine** | 150mcg | Near-universal gap during breastfeeding; required for thyroid peroxidase and infant brain development |

> **Iron absorption rules -- non-negotiable:**
> - Take iron on an **empty stomach** (or with just vitamin C)
> - No coffee, tea, or dairy within **1 hour** before or after iron
> - No calcium, magnesium, or zinc within **2 hours** of iron
> - Iron is every other day -- not daily

### Mid-Morning or Lunch (on iron days, after the 2-hour window)

| Supplement | Dose | Notes |
|------------|------|-------|
| **Zinc** | 8-12mg | Depleted by breastfeeding; competes with iron -- must be separated by 2+ hours |
| **CoQ10** (ubiquinol form) | 100-200mg | Meta-analysis confirmed fatigue reduction; expect 4-8 weeks for benefit |

### Evening (with dinner or before bed)

| Supplement | Dose | Notes |
|------------|------|-------|
| **Magnesium Glycinate** | 200-300mg | Take here -- separated from morning iron dose by 8+ hours |
| **L-Theanine** | 100-200mg | Best taken after final nursing session (half-life ~1 hour) |
| **Glycine** | 2-3g | Promotes deep NREM sleep via core temperature reduction |

## Diet Protocol

### Phase 1 -- Energy Restoration (Now -> Ferritin >50 ng/mL)

**Calorie target: 1,700-1,800 kcal/day**
At 15 months with comfort feeds only, lactation adds ~200-300 kcal/day demand. This is not a deficit phase -- it is a repletion phase. Undereating now extends fatigue.

**Protein target: 90g/day** (unchanged)
Practical rule: 250-300g chicken breast or lean red meat + 2-3 eggs daily.

**Iron-rich meal priority:**
Include at least one high-heme iron meal per day:
- Red meat (beef, lamb, kangaroo) -- highest heme density
- Chicken liver -- 11mg per 85g serving (exceptional)
- Beef liver -- 5mg per serving
- Kangaroo -- excellent iron + lean protein combination

Pair all iron-rich meals with a vitamin C source (capsicum, tomato, citrus, broccoli). Avoid tea or coffee with these meals.

**Breakfast (Mina eats breakfast -- Scott does not):**
Protein-first. Options:
- 2-3 scrambled eggs + lean meat (leftover batch-cooked chicken or beef)
- Greek yoghurt + berries (not on iron days if calcium is a concern -- swap to eggs)
- Do not rely on cornflakes as primary breakfast -- minimal protein, high glycaemic impact on energy

**Snacking strategy:**
Protein-first snacks over volume-based snacks. Options:
- Hard-boiled eggs (batch weekly)
- Sliced cooked chicken breast
- Beef jerky (home-made from Scott's batch)
- Full-fat Greek yoghurt

**Avoid on iron days with breakfast:**
- Coffee within 1 hour of iron supplement
- Milk or dairy within 1 hour
- Spinach-heavy smoothies (oxalates compete with iron)

### Phase 2 -- Moderate Deficit (Ferritin >50 ng/mL)

**Calorie target: 1,400-1,500 kcal/day**
This creates a ~300-400 kcal deficit -- safe during partial breastfeeding, sufficient for ~0.25-0.3 kg/week loss. Do not go below 1,400 kcal while still breastfeeding.

**Protein target: 90-100g/day** (hold or slightly increase as calories drop)

Weight loss target of 45 kg from 52 kg = 7 kg. At 0.25 kg/week, that is ~28 weeks from Phase 2 start. Realistic timeline: **October-November 2026** if Phase 2 begins in May-June 2026.

## Exercise Plan

### Phase 1 -- Build the Habit (Weeks 1-8)

Goal: establish consistency, not intensity. Fatigue is real -- do not push through it with high volume.

**Resistance Training -- 2x per week**
Full body, compound movements. 20-30 minutes.

*Session structure:*
- Goblet squat or bodyweight squat -- 3 sets
- Dumbbell Romanian deadlift -- 3 sets
- Dumbbell row (single arm) -- 3 sets each side
- Dumbbell shoulder press or push-up -- 3 sets
- Glute bridge or hip thrust -- 3 sets

*Intensity:* 2-3 RIR on all sets -- not to failure. Stimulus without cortisol spike. Save true failure sets for Phase 2.

*Timing:* Any time of day that fits. Not fasted -- train after breakfast on resistance days.

**Walking -- 4-5x per week**
20-40 minutes. Moderate pace. Morning preferred for circadian light exposure benefit.

**Total weekly volume: ~2 hours resistance + ~2.5 hours walking**

### Phase 2 -- Progressive Load (Ferritin >50 ng/mL)

Upgrade resistance training to 3x per week. Introduce progressive overload. One set per exercise can go to complete failure (final set only). Drop sets on isolation movements.

Add one HIIT session per week -- 20 minutes. Walking-based intervals sufficient.

## GP Referral -- Do This Now

| Test | Why |
|------|-----|
| **Ferritin** (not just haemoglobin) | Establish baseline; haemoglobin can be normal while ferritin is critically depleted |
| **TSH, free T4, free T3, TPO antibodies** | Postpartum thyroiditis affects up to 10% of women |
| **B12 and folate** | Confirm deficiency before supplementing |
| **25(OH)D (vitamin D)** | Confirm repletion target reached |
| **EPDS or PHQ-9 screening** | Iron deficiency raises depression risk by 53% |

Retest ferritin at **8 weeks**.

## Sleep Protocol (non-negotiable)

- **Morning light:** 20-30 min outdoor exposure within 1 hour of waking
- **Evening wind-down:** Dim lights after 8pm; blue-light filter on all screens
- **Warm shower/bath:** 10+ minutes at 40-42C, taken 90 minutes before bed -- reduces sleep onset latency by ~36%
- **Nighttime feeds:** Red or amber light only
- **Naps:** 10-20 minutes (alertness) or 90 minutes (full cycle)
- **Evening stack** (magnesium glycinate + L-theanine + glycine) taken 30-60 minutes before bed
