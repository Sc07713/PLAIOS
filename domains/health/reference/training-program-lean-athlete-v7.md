### Aesthetics-led LPP · longevity · basketball · mobility — Scott — June 2026

> Supersedes v6.3. Built from a **two-stage growth-optimisation pipeline** (Stage 1: dose-response growth model + greedy optimiser, tested + Codex/Gemini-validated; Stage 2: 5-model "virtual Scott" behavioural sim, run twice to convergence). Goals weighted **Aesthetics 40 / Longevity 20 / Basketball 20 / Mobility 20** — leanness + V-taper is the top lever. Study: `v7-growth-sim-pipeline.md`. ## DESIGN INTENT (4 goals, weighted)

| Goal (weight) | Served by | Where it lives |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| **Aesthetics 40** | upper hypertrophy (push + pull, to failure) + leanness | FIXED Tue + Thu |
| **Longevity 20** | strength, VO2 intervals, tissue resilience/armor | FIXED Mon + Tue conditioning finisher |
| **Basketball 20** | game, power/jumps/sprints, decel, skill | FIXED Mon power + Wed game |
| **Mobility 20** | loaded ROM + prehab, woven (not a separate day) | FIXED Mon warmup + armor |

**Aesthetic split (unchanged):** lean/powerful legs (RIR/explosive, NOT to failure, no hypertrophy volume — the anti-chafe lever) + hypertrophied upper body (to failure). **Leanness is ~90% diet** — the program maximises the training side; the *outcome* is gated on the deficit (see Nutrition + the parked testosterone layer).

| Day | Session | Tier | Contents |
|-----|---------|------|----------|
| **Mon** | LOWER + POWER | **FIXED** | t-spine/hip mobility · jumps + **2–3 short sprint/jump exposures** · leg strength (ATG split squat or Bulgarian, 2–3 RIR, explosive, NOT to failure) · single-leg RDL · armor (soleus, Copenhagen, Pallof) · decel ladder |
| **Tue** | PULL + engine | **FIXED** | Chin-ups/Pull-ups · DB Row · loaded carry · face pull · curls · **10–12 min 4×4 intervals finisher** |
| **Wed** | GAME | basketball | full-court (conditioning + skill + contact + COD); ankle prep first |
| **Thu** | PUSH | **FIXED** | Incline DB press (to failure) · Dips · Lateral raise · OHP · triceps · *(optional: a few rows/rear-delt for 2× back frequency)* |
| **Fri** | small pump | optional | laterals · curls · light rows · rear delt — **no failure pressing**, ~20 min |
| **Sat** | court + easy cardio | optional | shooting · post-up · easy Zone-2 *(no longer the load-bearing conditioning day)* |
| **Sun** | rest | — | — |

**Hitting Mon + Tue + Thu (+ the Wed game) = a successful week.** Those four carry every goal: aesthetics (Tue pull + Thu push), longevity (Mon strength + Tue intervals + armor), basketball (Mon power + Wed game), mobility (Mon). Fri/Sat are pure accelerant — skip with zero guilt.

## WHY IT'S BUILT THIS WAY (what the simulation found)

1. **V-taper on fixed days.** Pull is on the sticky early-week Tuesday (not a skipped reward day) so back/rear-delt — the most-neglected half of the V-taper — actually gets trained. Push on Thursday. Both to failure, double progression. *(Stage 2: putting the V-taper on fixed days lifted the aesthetics outcome 3→4 across most models — "design intent achieved.")*
2. **Conditioning on a fixed day.** The 4×4 VO2 dose is a 10–12 min finisher on Tuesday — NOT stranded on the optional Saturday (where the re-sim showed longevity collapsing to ~1–2/5). *(Stage 2 v7.1→v7.2 fix: 4/5 models said pull conditioning onto a fixed day.)*
3. **Sprints seeded on Monday, not a cold Saturday block.** 2–3 short exposures woven into the power day adapt the tendons gradually — removes the "cold sprint once a week" calf/Achilles injury setup the sim kept flagging.
4. **Armor stays a non-negotiable micro-dose on Monday** — near-zero offensive value (which is *why* it gets skipped), kept as high-priority injury insurance.
5. **Frequency nudge:** a few back/rear-delt sets on Thursday give the back ~2× weekly frequency (Tue heavy + Thu light) — the research-favoured frequency for a 40%-weighted hypertrophy goal — without a 4th fixed day.

## SET / REP SCHEME

- **Upper aesthetics (Tue pull, Thu push):** 3 sets, last to failure, double progression (hit top reps on last set → add load).
- **Lower (Mon):** 3 sets, 3–5 reps, **2–3 RIR, explosive — NOT to failure** (lean/power, not hypertrophy).
- **Armor (Mon):** small non-negotiable micro-dose.
- **Conditioning (Tue):** 4×4 (4 × [4 min @90–95% HRmax / 3 min easy]) — or Zone-2/3×3 during the ramp.
- **Skill:** piggybacked — game (Wed) + court (Sat) + daily home dribbling.
- **RUSH (short on time):** 2 sets, top movements. **EMERGENCY (<12 min):** 1 set, the compounds.

## RE-ENTRY RAMP (post-RSV + post-fast) — criteria-gated, lands first

The week above is the steady-state TARGET. Ramp into it; don't start here.

| Phase | Lifting | Conditioning | Power/speed | Gate to advance |
|-------|---------|--------------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Wk 1–2** | 3 sessions, RPE 5–6, **no failure**, 2–3 RIR everywhere | Zone-2 / 3×3 only — no 4×4 | build-up strides 70–80%, easy pogos, landings — **no max sprints/depth jumps** | symptom-free + green recovery markers 7 straight days |
| **Wk 3** | add 3rd set; failure on low-risk isolation only | one 4×4 **or** sprint prep — not both | true acceleration sprints; lateral bounds | no next-morning tendon/hamstring soreness |
| **Wk 4+** | full (upper to failure, lower RIR) | full 4×4 finisher | seed sprints on Mon; approach jumps | 20 pain-free single-leg calf raises/side + clean stick landings |

**Recovery gate:** maintenance calories — **no deficit** — until resting HR, sleep, appetite, breathing, and session RPE are normal for 7 straight days. Stop for chest pain, undue breathlessness, palpitations, or syncope.

## NUTRITION & SLEEP (the real aesthetics lever)

- **Leanness is ~90% diet.** The training maximises the muscle side; the *look* is gated on the deficit. The sim is explicit: every run that held the deficit got the lean result; every run that didn't, didn't.
- Maintenance until immune-recovered (ramp gate), then 400–500 kcal/day deficit, moderate.
- Protein ~200 g/day (2.0 g/kg).
- Sleep: 7+ hrs target (not a 10 PM cage — it competes with the only couple/inbox time and reads as a stressor).
- **Highest-leverage parked build:** testosterone + nutrition protocol. The training profile (compound, posterior-chain, adequate-not-excess volume, leanness) already pulls T the right way; measuring + the nutrition layer is the next unlock.
