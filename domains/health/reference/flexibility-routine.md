# Complete Daily Flexibility Routine - Martial Arts Enhanced
*42 minutes total | 15s x 4 sets with 10s rest = optimal flexibility gains*
*Integrated Wushu stretches for basketball-specific movement*

---

## Dynamic Warm-Up (3 min)
- Leg swings front/back + side-to-side: 10 each direction, each leg
- Arm circles: 10 forward, 10 backward
- Hip circles: 10 each direction
- Walking lunges with twist: 6 each leg
- Horse stance pulses: 30 seconds

## Upper Body (7.5 min)
- Doorway Chest Stretch: 15s x 4 each arm
- Cross-Body Shoulder Stretch: 15s x 4 each arm
- Overhead Tricep Stretch: 15s x 4 each arm
- Upper Trap/Neck Stretch: 15s x 4 each side
- Wrist/Forearm Stretch: 15s x 4 each arm

## Core, Back & Rotational Power (9 min)
- Child's Pose: 15s x 4
- Frog to Child's Pose Flow: 15s x 4 transitions (hip abduction to flexion)
- Cobra/Upward Dog: 15s x 4
- Scorpion Stretch: 15s x 4 each side (layup mechanics, shot blocking)
- Seated Spinal Twist: 15s x 4 each side

## Lower Body - Standing & Dynamic Balance (10.5 min)
- Standing Quad Stretch: 15s x 4 each leg
- Crane Stance Prep: 15s x 4 each leg (single-leg drives, defensive recovery)
- Calf Stretch: 15s x 4 each leg
- Warrior III Preparation: 15s x 4 each leg (single-leg balance, defensive reach)
- IT Band Stretch: 15s x 4 each leg
- Runner's Lunge: 15s x 4 each leg

## Lower Body - Wide Stance & Power Positions (12 min)
- Horse Stance with Rotation: 30s hold + 15s rotations each direction x 2 sets
- Cossack Squat: 15s x 4 each leg
- Dragon Squat: 15s x 4 each side (deep squat + torso rotation)
- Wide-Legged Forward Fold: 15s x 4
- Side Split Attempt: 15s x 4

## Lower Body - Seated & Deep Hip Work (15 min)
- Standing Hamstring Stretch: 15s x 4 each leg
- Seated Side Reach Hamstring Stretch: 15s x 4 each leg
- Lotus/Half-Lotus Progression: 15s x 4 each leg (never force rotation)
- Butterfly Stretch: 15s x 4
- Lotus to Forward Fold: 15s x 4 transitions
- Front Split Attempt: 15s x 4 each leg
- Hip Flexor Stretch: 15s x 4 each leg

---

## Basketball Transfer Summary
- **Defensive:** Horse stance + Lotus = wider stance; Dragon/Cossack = stay low; Crane + Warrior III = single-leg recovery
- **Offensive:** Scorpion + Warrior III = layup reach; Frog flow = rapid scrambling; Lotus fold = explosive starts
- **Change of Direction:** Horse rotation = core stability; Dragon transitions = stance changes; Crane = cutting control

## Progression Timeline
- Week 1-2: Horse stance and Crane stance only
- Week 3-4: Add Dragon squat, Frog to Child's flow
- Week 5-8: Lotus progressions, Scorpion stretch
- Month 3+: Warrior III, Lotus to forward fold

## Usage Note (from LPP v5.3)
No structured sessions required. Use informally during TV time, post-training if tight, or after Wednesday VO2 max run. Consistency of casual stretching beats occasional dedicated sessions.
