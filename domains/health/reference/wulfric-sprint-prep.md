# Evidence-Based Sprint Preparation for Youth Beginner
*5-week protocol for 12-13yo male (Wulfric) with no sprint background*

## Key Finding
Acceleration distance is the only statistically significant predictor of 100m time in adolescent males (Bleeker & Siener 2025, p<0.001). U14 boys accelerate over ~18-20m, maintain top speed ~25-30m, then spend 40-50m decelerating.

## Training Priority
Neural adaptations drive sprint improvement at this age, not muscle growth. A 52% gain in leg strength produced only 2.5% improvement in 30m sprint time in youth athletes.

## Recommended Protocol (Moran et al. 2017)
Twice-weekly, up to 16 sprints of ~20m, work-to-rest ratio at least 1:25. ES = 1.01 overall.

## Teaching Progression
1. Upright mechanics first (posture, arm drive, knee lift, elastic ground contact)
2. Then acceleration postures
3. Standing starts faster than blocks for beginners
4. Arm drive: "eye socket to hip pocket -- switch"
5. Foot strike: forefoot, dorsiflexed, under/behind center of mass
6. Acceleration lean from ankles, not waist (55-70 degrees for youth)

## Essential Drills
- Wall drives (2x8 each leg)
- A-march (2x20m)
- A-skip (2x20m)
- B-skip (2x20m)
- Wicket runs (6-10 mini-hurdles at 5-5.5ft spacing)
- Falling starts (3-4x10-15m)

## 5-Week Block Periodization

### Weeks 1-2: Movement Foundations
- Session A: 6-8x20m at 75-85%, sprint drills, falling starts. Volume: 150-250m
- Session B: 3-point start practice 4-6x20m, 3-4x30m, basic plyometrics. Volume: 200-300m
- Session C: Drill circuit, 6x100m at 60-65%, baseline 60m time trial end Week 2

### Weeks 3-4: Speed Development
- Session A: 4x30m at 90-95%, 3x50m at 85-90%. Volume: ~270m high intensity
- Session B: Start practice, 100m time trial at 90-95%, Week 4 adds 200m trial
- Session C: 3x60m curve work, 2x80-100m at 85%, 3x100m tempo. Volume: 500-600m mixed

### Week 5: Taper
- Volume drops 40-60%, intensity stays 90-100%
- Session A: 3x30m at 95%, 1x60m at 90%
- Session B: 3x40m build-ups, 1x30m at 95%, visualization

## 100m vs 200m
- 100m: ~80% anaerobic, 20% aerobic. All-out from gun.
- 200m: ~72% anaerobic, 28% aerobic. Controlled acceleration through curve, open up on straight.
- Curve: rotate hips to align with lane, "headlights following the road," run center of lane

## Managing Three Training Streams
- Reduce Norwegian 4x4 from 2x/week to 1x/week during sprint block
- Never sprint within 24 hours of VO2 max session
- 48+ hours between sprint and heavy lower-body resistance
- Cap total high-intensity sessions at 4/week
- Resistance program shifts to sprint-support: lower-body volume reduced 30-40%, explosive intent (3-5 reps)

## Weekly Schedule
| Day | Session | Intensity |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Sprint (acceleration/max velocity) | High |
| Tuesday | Resistance (upper focus or full-body maintenance) | Moderate |
| Wednesday | Norwegian 4x4 VO2 max | High |
| Thursday | Rest or active recovery | Low |
| Friday | Sprint (speed endurance/200m-specific) | High |
| Saturday | Resistance (lower body reduced, power emphasis) | Moderate-High |
| Sunday | Complete rest | None |

## Safety (Adolescent)
- PHV average ~13.8 years -- heightened injury vulnerability
- Monitor: Sever's disease (calcaneal), Osgood-Schlatter (tibial tubercle)
- Total training hours should not exceed child's age per week (12-13 max)
- Sleep: minimum 9-10 hours
- 15-25g protein within 2 hours post-training

## RAMP Warm-Up (18-22 min)
- Raise (4 min): light jog, skips, shuffles, carioca
- Activate/Mobilize (6 min): leg swings, lunges, hip circles, glute bridges
- Potentiate (8 min): A-march, A-skip, B-skip, high knees, progressive build-ups (60% -> 75% -> 85% -> 95%)
- NO static stretching before sprinting (impairs sprint times in 13-15yo)

## Realistic Expectations
- Untrained 12-13yo: 15-19s for 100m
- Athletic untrained: 13-15s for 100m
- 5-week improvement: 0.5-1.5s in 100m, 1.0-3.0s in 200m
- Hand-timed results run 0.2-0.3s faster than electronic
