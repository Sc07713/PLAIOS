## When to Refer

### Immediate Medical Attention
- Head injury/concussion symptoms (especially Wulfric)
- Cardiac irregularity during fasting or training
- Fainting or loss of consciousness
- Joint instability, significant swelling, or loss of function
- Allergic reaction to any supplement

### GP Consultation
- Symptoms beyond expected recovery timelines
- Suspected hormonal dysfunction despite optimized lifestyle (3-6 months)
- Pediatric growth falling below/crossing WHO percentile lines
- Adverse supplement reaction (discontinue first)
- Overtraining syndrome not resolved by deload
- Mina: mastitis, supply concerns, deficiency symptoms
- Audrey: RED-S signs or stress fractures

### Specialist Referral
- Endocrinologist: Testosterone optimization insufficient after 3-6 months lifestyle optimization
- Sports medicine: Persistent musculoskeletal issues
- Pediatric developmental: Regression or new concerns with Evie
- Registered dietitian: If breastfeeding + weight loss proves medically complex for Mina
