## Height Optimization -- Evidence-Based

### What Works
1. Genetics (primary, but expression depends on environment)
2. Protein intake (adequate complete protein)
3. Sleep quality + duration (9+ hours, GH in deep sleep)
4. Vitamin D status
5. Zinc adequacy (GH receptor signaling)
6. Absence of chronic inflammation

### What Doesn't Work (Debunked)
- Royal jelly (cross-species extrapolation fallacy)
- Pressure points (no mechanism)
- K-pop diets (restrictive, inappropriate for growing children)

### Implementation
- 7pm screen cutoff (protects melatonin and GH pulses)
- No sugar after dinner (insulin suppresses GH)
- Compound resistance training (stimulates GH)
- Consistent protein at every meal
