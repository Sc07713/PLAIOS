## Weekly Meal Structure

### Prep Day: Sunday

### Busy Days (Mon, Tue, Wed, Fri)
- **Breakfast (kids + Mina):** Eggs + toast, or cereal + milk. Scott fasts.
- **Lunch:** 200g chicken breast + 3 eggs template
- **Dinner:** Slow cooker or quick stir-fries

### Rotation
- Red meat: 2x/week minimum
- Fish: 1x/week minimum
- Chicken: 3-4x/week (cost-effective staple)
- Kangaroo: Occasional (highest iron, leanest)

## Grocery Budget
- **Target:** $180-200/week
- **Meat:** ~14kg/week (largest item)
- **Eggs:** ~4 dozen/week minimum
- **Suppliers:** Coles, Woolworths. BulkSupplements for supplements, VPA Australia for speed.

## Milk Decision
Optimal: Jersey, non-homogenised, full cream, A2, grass-fed. Brands: Schulz Organic, Paris Creek. Intact MFGM supports neurological development.

## Cooking Techniques
- Slow cooker: busy day staple
- Oven: silverside at 140-150C 3hrs
- Air fryer: chicken breast, jerky
- Jerky: silverside or kangaroo, 2-3mm, 100C then 50C
- Fermented: kimchi (gochugaru, fish sauce, wombok), sauerkraut
