## MODE SELECTION

| Mode | When to Use | Time |
|------|-------------|------|
| **FULL** | Standard training days | 25-42 min |
| **RUSH** | CEO crunch, life happens | 12-18 min |

**Rush Rule:** ONE set per exercise to failure, 60-second rest, hold final eccentric. Same tempo. No warm-up sets. That's it.

**Full Rule:** All prescribed sets. Sets 1-2 at 2-3 RIR, final set to failure. 2-minute rest on strength days, 60-90 seconds on hypertrophy days.

---

## TEMPO -- EVERY RESISTANCE EXERCISE, BOTH MODES

- **LOWER:** 3-4 seconds -- controlled, feel the muscle stretch
- **PAUSE:** 1-2 seconds at the bottom -- dead stop, no bounce
- **LIFT:** 1-2 seconds -- fast with intent
- **No pause at top** -- reverse smoothly and start lowering again

## VO2 MAX -- NORWEGIAN 4x4 PROTOCOL

**Why this exists:** VO2 max is the single strongest predictor of all-cause mortality. Low fitness carries 5x the mortality risk of smoking. Each 1-MET improvement = 13-15% mortality reduction. No upper limit of benefit found.

### Sunday -- Run at Keilor Park
*While Audrey trains at Keilor Stadium. Zero additional time cost.*

| Phase | Duration | Intensity | What It Feels Like |
|-------|----------|-----------|-------------------|
| Warm-up | 10 min | 60-70% HRmax | Easy jog, conversation pace |
| **Interval 1** | **4 min** | **90-95% HRmax** | **Can't speak in sentences. RPE 8-9/10.** |
| Recovery 1 | 3 min | 70% HRmax | Light jog, catch breath |
| **Interval 2** | **4 min** | **90-95% HRmax** | **Same intensity. Legs burning.** |
| Recovery 2 | 3 min | 70% HRmax | Light jog |
| **Interval 3** | **4 min** | **90-95% HRmax** | **Mental game starts here.** |
| Recovery 3 | 3 min | 70% HRmax | Light jog |
| **Interval 4** | **4 min** | **90-95% HRmax** | **Everything you've got left.** |
| Cool-down | 3-5 min | Easy | Walk it off |

**Total: ~35-40 minutes. 16 minutes at near-max cardiac output.**

### Wednesday -- Run at Football Field
*All sessions running-based. Running HIIT produces roughly double the visceral fat reduction of cycling (effect size -0.44 vs -0.21, Maillard et al. 2018).*

Same intervals, same intensities, same recoveries. Local football field -- flat, predictable surface, no traffic.

### Friday -- Run at Football Field
*Third session captures full dose-response for visceral fat. Pushes above the 10 METs-h/week threshold (Ohkawara et al. 2007). Friday is Push Hypertrophy (upper body) -- zero leg interference from morning run.*

Same protocol. Morning run, then push hypertrophy session after.

### VO2 Max Rules

1. **Use your Polar H10 chest strap** -- paired to Garmin Instinct via ANT+. Real-time accuracy to hit 90-95% HRmax.
2. **Find your max heart rate first** -- Warm up 10 min, do 2 full intervals, then sprint to absolute exhaustion at the 2-min mark of interval 3. Highest number = your HRmax. Standard formula (220 - age = 182) is a starting estimate only.
3. **90-95% HRmax is the target zone** -- Below 90% and the cardiac stimulus is insufficient. Above 95% and you can't sustain 4 minutes.
4. **Intervals are sustained hard effort, not sprints** -- Think "fastest pace you could hold for 4 minutes" not "all-out 20-second burst." The point is time at maximal cardiac output.
5. **Don't skip warm-up** -- The 10-minute warm-up is functional, not optional. Cold-start intervals spike injury risk and compromise performance.
6. **Sunday runs go before Legs Strength** -- Minimum 6 hours between VO2 max and resistance work. Morning run at Keilor, afternoon/evening legs at home.
7. **Wednesday run before Thursday legs** -- Running is cardiovascular stress, not muscular damage. Thursday's high-rep hypertrophy work won't be meaningfully compromised. If first 1-2 sets feel heavier, push through -- it normalises.
8. **Friday run before Push Hypertrophy** -- Entirely different muscle groups. Morning run, then upper body session. Zero interference.

### Progression
- **Weeks 1-2:** Hit the protocol. Don't worry about pace. Focus on sustaining 90-95% HRmax for all 4 intervals.
- **Weeks 3-4:** Pace/resistance should naturally increase as cardiac efficiency improves.
- **Week 6+:** Expect measurable VO2 max improvement (6-10% typical in research).
- **Maintenance:** Once at target fitness, 1 session/week maintains. 2 sessions/week continues improvement.

> **Standing KB Hamstring Curl Setup:** Kettlebell tied to rope, attached to back of foot/heel. Stand on plyo box for clearance so KB hangs freely. Hold box or wall for balance. Curl heel toward glute -- 3-4s up, 1-2s squeeze at top, 3-4s lowering. Single leg, each side to failure.

> **Path back to Nordics:** When standing curls get easy at 24kg and you're repping past 20+, hamstrings are strong enough for eccentric-only Nordics. Or swap to machine hamstring curl once gym membership happens.

## MONDAY -- PUSH STRENGTH

| # | Exercise | Full | Rush | Notes |
|---|----------|------|------|-------|
| 1 | Incline DB Press | 3 x ~6 | 1 x fail | 30-45 degree incline. Hold final eccentric. |
| 2 | DB Overhead Press | 3 x ~6 | 1 x fail | Hold final eccentric. |
| 3 | Lateral Raise | 2 x 15-20 | 1 x fail | High reps always. |
| 4 | Skull Crusher | 2 x ~6 | 1 x fail | Long head -- stretched position. Hold final eccentric. |
| 5 | Reverse Press (pronated) | 2 x ~6 | 1 x fail | Lateral/medial heads -- go heavier than skull crushers. |

> **Why split triceps on strength day:** Skull crushers load the long head in its weakest stretched position. Reverse press loads the lateral/medial heads in the strongest pressing position. Combined, your weight is limited by the weaker exercise -- the press never gets properly loaded. Splitting lets you go heavier where you're stronger.

> **Incline press angle:** 30-45 degrees. All direct chest work is now incline -- clavicular head is the aesthetic differentiator for V-taper. The sternal head gets sufficient indirect activation from pressing at any angle. Underdeveloped lower chest is essentially non-existent in natural lifters.

> **Reverse press form:** Lying on bench, dumbbells at waist level, knuckles facing feet (pronated grip). Press straight to ceiling. Pronated grip shifts activation to the medial head -- the most under-developed head in most lifters.

> **Lateral raises stay high-rep always.** Predominantly slow-twitch muscle. Heavy lateral raises wreck the shoulder joint for minimal benefit.

## TUESDAY -- PULL STRENGTH

| # | Exercise | Full | Rush | Notes |
|---|----------|------|------|-------|
| 1 | Pull-ups | 3 x fail | 1 x fail | Every set to failure. Full dead hang. |
| 2 | DB Row | 3 x ~6 each | 1 x fail each | Hold final eccentric. |
| 3 | Rear Delt Fly | 2 x 15-20 | 1 x fail | High reps always. |
| 4 | Standing Hammer Curl | 3 x ~6 | 1 x fail | Neutral grip. Brachialis + biceps long head. Hold final eccentric. |
| 5 | Wrist Curl -> Reverse Wrist Curl | 2 x ~6 each | 1 x fail each | Back-to-back, no rest between. Flexors then extensors. |

> **Rear delt flyes stay high-rep always.** Same logic as lateral raises. Small muscle, better served by volume and control. Also keeps shoulders healthy by balancing pressing.

> **Hammer curls standing on strength day.** Neutral grip loads brachialis (which doesn't cross the shoulder) -- no benefit from lying back. Heavier load, compound-ish movement. Save the bench for Saturday's supinated curls where the stretch actually matters.

> **Forearm finisher:** Wrist curls (palms up) straight into reverse wrist curls (palms down), no rest between. One position, flip grip, done. 90 seconds total.

> **When weight gets too light:** Stack 1.5 reps (full down, half up, full down, full up = 1 rep) + 4-5s eccentrics + 3-4s pause at bottom. If all exhausted, hold two dumbbells in front rack instead of goblet position for double the load.

## FRIDAY -- PUSH HYPERTROPHY

| # | Exercise | Full | Rush | Notes |
|---|----------|------|------|-------|
| 1 | Incline DB Press | 3 x 15-20 | 1 x fail + drop | 4s eccentrics. |
| 2 | Incline DB Chest Fly | 3 x 15-20 | 1 x fail + drop | 30-45 degree incline. Deep stretch at bottom. |
| 3 | Lateral Raise | 3 x 15-20 | 1 x fail + drop | Last set to failure + drop set. |
| 4 | Skull Crusher -> Reverse Press Flow | 3 x 15-20 | 1 x fail + drop | Combined movement -- see notes. |

> **Skull Crusher -> Reverse Press Flow:** One continuous set. Skull crusher up, rotate to pronated grip, lower to hips (chambered fist position), press to ceiling, reverse back. On hypertrophy day the lighter weight doesn't penalise the press -- you're chasing metabolic stress, not max load.

> **OHP stays Monday-only.** Incline press + fly already exhaust front delts. Lateral raises twice a week for V-taper width. No redundant pressing.

> **Incline fly form:** Set bench to 30-45 degrees. Lower dumbbells with slight elbow bend until deep stretch across chest. The incline shifts tension to the clavicular head -- same stretch benefit as flat, better upper chest bias. Don't clang at top -- stop just short of dumbbells touching and reverse. The stretch is where the growth happens.

## SATURDAY -- PULL HYPERTROPHY

| # | Exercise | Full | Rush | Notes |
|---|----------|------|------|-------|
| 1 | DB Pullover | 3 x 15-20 | 1 x fail | Deep stretch behind head. |
| 2 | DB Row | 3 x 15-20 each | 1 x fail + drop | Last set to failure + drop set. |
| 3 | Rear Delt Fly | 3 x 15-20 | 1 x fail + drop | Last set to failure + drop set. |
| 4 | Lying Supinated Curl | 3 x 15-20 | 1 x fail + drop | Lie back on bench, arms hanging behind torso. Deep long head stretch. |
| 5 | Standing Reverse Curl | 2 x 15-20 | 1 x fail | Pronated grip. Brachialis + forearm extensors. |
| 6 | Wrist Curl -> Reverse Wrist Curl | 2 x 15-20 each | 1 x fail each | Back-to-back, no rest between. Flexors then extensors. |

> **Pullover form:** Lie across bench, hips low. Lower dumbbell behind head with slightly bent elbows until deep lat stretch. Pull back over chest. Best lat hypertrophy exercise without a cable machine.

> **Lying supinated curl:** Lie back on incline bench (or flat bench with shoulders at edge), arms hanging behind torso. This pre-stretches the biceps long head across the shoulder joint -- maximum tension at maximum length. Supinated grip is the key pairing here; the long head responds to this stretched position. Hammer and reverse grip don't benefit from the lie-back position because the brachialis doesn't cross the shoulder.

> **Reverse curl form:** Standing, pronated grip (palms down), strict curl. Hits brachialis and forearm extensors -- the top of the forearm. Lighter than regular curls; ego stays at the door.

> **Wrist curl form:** Sit on bench, forearms resting on thighs, wrists hanging off knees. Curl the weight by flexing the wrist. Full range of motion -- let the dumbbell roll to fingertips at the bottom, curl all the way up. Hits forearm flexors -- the meaty underside.

> **Reverse wrist curl:** Same position, flip to palms-down. Extend the wrist upward. Lighter weight than regular wrist curls -- extensors are smaller. Pair them back-to-back with no rest for a complete forearm burnout.

## The Rules

### Strength Days (Sunday PM, Monday, Tuesday)
- Heavy weight, ~6 reps (Full) or to failure (Rush)
- Rest 2 min (Full) or 60 sec (Rush)
- Last set to failure, hold final eccentric on compounds
- Lateral raises and rear delt flyes stay high-rep (15-20) always
- Triceps split: skull crushers (long head) + reverse press (lateral/medial) -- separate weights

### Hypertrophy Days (Thursday, Friday, Saturday)
- Lighter weight, 15-20 reps
- Rest 60-90 sec (Full) or 60 sec (Rush)
- Last set to failure + drop set
- Triceps combined: skull crusher -> reverse press flow -- one continuous movement

### VO2 Max Days (Sunday AM, Wednesday, Friday AM)
- Norwegian 4x4 protocol -- 4 intervals of 4 minutes at 90-95% HRmax
- **All sessions running-based** -- running HIIT produces ~2x visceral fat reduction vs cycling
- Sunday: Run at Keilor Park (while Audrey trains)
- Wednesday: Run at football field
- Friday: Run at football field (before Push Hypertrophy)
- Polar H10 chest strap paired to Garmin Instinct via ANT+

## When Weight Gets Too Light

| # | Strategy | Detail |
|---|----------|--------|
| 1 | 1.5 reps | Full rep + half rep = 1 rep. Doubles TUT. |
| 2 | Slow eccentrics | Extend lowering to 4-5 seconds. |
| 3 | Extend pause | Increase bottom pause to 3-4 seconds. |
| 4 | Go unilateral | Single-arm/leg. Effectively doubles load. |
| 5 | Rest-pause | Fail, rest 10s, go again, rest 10s, go again. |
| 6 | Buy heavier gear | Only when all above are exhausted. |
