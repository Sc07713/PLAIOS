# Testosterone Decline & Recovery Research

## Population-Wide Decline
- ~1% decrease per year since 1970s, age-independent, global
- Massachusetts Male Aging Study: 60yo men 17.5 nmol/L (1987) vs 15 nmol/L (2002)
- Particularly affects young men aged 15-39
- Cannot be explained by obesity/smoking alone -- environmental factors

## Elite Athletes
- 25.4% of elite male athletes have testosterone below 10 nmol/L (GH-2000 Olympic study, 689 athletes)
- Endurance athletes: up to 30% reduction after 5+ years training
- Exercise-Hypogonadal Male Condition (EHMC) affects 15-25% of male endurance athletes
- Marathon/cycling worst affected; basketball/alpine skiing relatively preserved

## Environmental Factors
- EDCs: global plastic production 50 -> 300 million tons since 1970s
- Phthalates: 10.8-24% testosterone decline in exposed populations
- BPA directly disrupts testosterone synthesis in Leydig cells

## Training Impact
- Overtraining: testosterone drops from 6.8+/-1.0 to 4.4+/-1.0 ng/ml
- Year-round competition eliminates traditional recovery periods
- International travel disrupts circadian rhythms crucial for hormone production

## Optimization Strategies
- Sleep: minimum 3-hour uninterrupted blocks with normal REM architecture
- 5 hours/night for 1 week = 10-15% testosterone decrease (equivalent to 10-20 years aging)
- Nutrition: 20-35% calories from healthy fats; low-fat diets decrease testosterone 0.38 SD
- Micronutrients: zinc, vitamin D, magnesium, B12
- Resistance training with compound movements: acute boosts lasting 15-60 minutes
- Testosterone-to-cortisol ratio as key biomarker; monitor every 6-12 weeks via LC-MS

## Recovery & Addiction Research (Pornography)
- Dopamine release up to 250% above normal
- Reduced gray matter in prefrontal cortex
- Recovery timeline: initial improvements ~90 days, dopamine receptor upregulation ~day 30
- Significant prefrontal cortex healing 3-6 months
- Complete neurological recovery ~14 months
- ACT therapy: 92% reduction in viewing after 12 sessions (Utah State)
- Exercise: 31% anxiety reduction, 47% depression reduction
- NAC 600-1200mg/day reduces cravings across addiction types
- "90-day reboot" supported by brain imaging
