## Core Principles

### Nutrition
1. **Protein consistency > aggressive fasting** -- MPS is pulsatile, can't be "caught up." WHY Scott moved from OMAD to 2MAD.
2. **"500g meat + 3 eggs" rule** -- simple, memorable, accommodates executive demands.
3. **Standard RDA protein is insufficient** for active, growing children. Dramatic growth spurts observed after implementing higher intakes.
4. **Sustainability > aggression** -- strategic flexibility prevents binge-restrict cycles.
5. **Daily protein consistency > weekly extended fasting** for body composition.

### Supplements
6. **Cost-effectiveness drives choices** -- bulk powders, calculate cost per day.
7. **Weekend cycling prevents dopaminergic tolerance** -- DLPA, rhodiola, alpha-GPC OFF weekends.
8. **Subjective experience is valid data** -- DLPA > tyrosine (feels better), mucuna rejected (feels awful).
9. **Don't stack redundant cortisol modulators** -- ashwagandha baseline; PS and magnolia acute tools.
10. **Quarterly extended fasts = full system resets** -- neurochemical, metabolic, spiritual, digestive.

### Family Health
11. **Sleep is the PRIMARY lever** for nearly everything.
12. **Penny's sleep disruptions are #1 constraint** on Scott's progress.
13. **Correcting genuine nutritional gaps produces dramatic results** -- children weren't previously well-nourished.
14. **Screen cutoff 7pm for older children** -- protects melatonin, deep sleep, GH pulses.

## Key Learnings From Experience

### Fasting
- Autophagy meaningful only days 4-7
- 1.3-1.5kg actual fat over 7 days (vs 4-7kg scale)
- Gout risk: ketosis -> uric acid. Manage with hydration, baking soda, vitamin C
- Gallbladder dormant after extended fast -- refeeding with fat causes reflux
- Day 2 hardest, Day 3+ progressively easier

### Nutrition
- Cafe breakfasts are protein-deceptive (tier system prevents counting illusions)
- Chicken breast cheaper than thighs in AU
- Kangaroo = best combo of cost, iron, lean protein
- 1-2kg daily weight fluctuations are normal

### Supplements
- Mag bisglycinate powder tastes terrible -- use tablets
- PS powder is 20% complex (500mg powder = 100mg active)
- P5P is a missing cofactor in dopaminergic stack
- Rhodiola + DLPA synergy is real (MAO inhibition extends dopamine half-life)
