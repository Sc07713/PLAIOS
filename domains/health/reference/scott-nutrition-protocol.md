# Scott's Nutrition, Fat Loss & Training Protocol

- **Start:** ~100kg (milestone hit late January 2026)
- **Target:** 80kg with visible abs
- **Deadline:** Wedding, June 6, 2026
- **Realistic projection:** 11-12% body fat by wedding (not full 10%)
- **Rate:** ~0.7-1.0 kg/week fat loss
- **Current status:** Simultaneously gaining muscle (muscle memory) while losing fat -- creatine water retention masks true fat loss on scale

## Daily Nutrition Framework

### The Core Rule: "500g meat + 3 eggs"

**Meal 1 (Lunch):** 250g meat + 2-3 eggs = ~80-90g protein
**Meal 2 (Dinner):** 250g meat + 2-3 eggs = ~50-70g protein
**Optional evening:** Protein shake with milk as wind-down ritual
**Daily total:** 150-180g protein at ~1,300-1,800 calories

### Protein Density Reference (per 100g cooked)
| Source | Protein | Fat | Notes |
|--------|---------|-----|-------|
| Chicken breast | 31g | 3.6g | Cheapest AND most protein-dense in AU |
| Kangaroo | 23g | 2g | Best lean option, highest iron, great for jerky |
| Silverside/topside | 25-28g | 4-6g | $12-16/kg, excellent for roasting + meal prep |
| Chuck steak | 26g | 10g | Higher fat, good slow cooker option |
| Lamb leg | 25g | 8g | Good for family roasts |
| Eggs (each) | 6g | 5g | Scott's cheapest protein source |

### Protein Tier System for Counting
- **Tier 1 -- Full count:** Whole cuts (steak, chicken breast, fish fillets)
- **Tier 2 -- 30% discount:** Marinated or bone-in cuts. 200g bone-in chicken ribs = 130g actual meat.
- **Tier 3 -- Don't count:** Processed meats (bacon, sausages, deli meats)

### Calorie Budget
- **Estimated TDEE:** 2,400-2,600 cal/day
- **Target intake:** ~1,300-1,800 cal/day
- **Target deficit:** ~770 cal/day for ~0.1kg/day loss

### Nutritional Hierarchy (Priority Order)
1. **Protein** -- 150-160g daily, non-negotiable
2. **Fiber** -- Psyllium husk (5-10g, 30 min before lunch with 400ml water)
3. **Fats** -- Come naturally from meat sources
4. **Vitamins** -- Covered by supplement stack
5. **Carbs** -- Minimal, incidental from vegetables

### Foods That Impede Fat Loss (Worst First)
1. Seed oils -- mitochondrial dysfunction, inflammatory omega-6
2. Fructose/sugar -- hepatic de novo lipogenesis
3. Alcohol -- complete metabolic halt
4. Sleep deprivation -- hormonal disruption
5. Carb-fat combinations -- hyperpalatable, drive overconsumption
6. Constant snacking -- elevated insulin prevents fat mobilization
7. Cornstarch/refined glucose -- temporary glucose spikes

## Fasting Architecture

### Daily: 2MAD (Two Meals A Day)
- Lunch-to-dinner eating window (~6-8 hours)
- Fasted morning training
- First meal breaks fast with protein priority

### Reactive: 24-36 Hour Fasts
- After indulgences as "caloric periodization"
- NOT guilt-driven restriction -- strategic caloric banking
- Skip when sleep debt is high (cortisol compounding)

### Quarterly: 5-7 Day Extended Fasts
- Aligned with school holidays
- Primary purpose: Autophagy (meaningful only days 4-7), metabolic reset, spiritual practice, dopaminergic system reset
- Actual fat loss: 1.3-1.5kg over 7 days (vs 4-7kg scale)
- First fast completed January 8-12, 2026 (5 days)
- Target: 40+ fasting days annually

### Extended Fast Lessons Learned
- Day 2 is hardest (gout from uric acid, GI issues)
- Gout resolved by Day 3 with hydration + baking soda + vitamin C
- Day 3+ brings profound clarity
- "Leave the table when you're up" -- end on a positive
- Refeeding: LEAN PROTEIN FIRST (dormant gallbladder). Egg drop soup ideal.
- 2-3 resistance sessions sufficient for muscle preservation

## Key Fat Loss Decisions

1. Protein consistency > aggressive fasting (MPS is pulsatile)
2. 2MAD > OMAD (protein distribution limits)
3. Sustainability > aggression (prevents binge-restrict cycles)
4. NEAT before formal cardio
5. Save yohimbine for lean phase
6. Forskolin primarily for thyroid support
7. Sleep debt is the primary constraint (Penny's disruptions -> cortisol -> fat storage)
