### Strength · Hypertrophy · Conditioning · Basketball — Scott — May 2026

> Supersedes LPP v5.4. Basketball-performance overlay on a longevity base, with an explicit aesthetic split: **lean/powerful legs, hypertrophied upper body.** v6.1 incorporated a Codex + Gemini review; v6.2 was adherence-hardened from a 5-model "virtual Scott" simulation + a movement ROI index; **v6.3 is value-optimised from an equal-weight (33/33/33) cross-domain combination simulation** (see `v6-value-combination-sim-equal-weights.md`, `v6-movement-index-and-simulation.md`, `journal/decisions/2026-05-31-training-value-optimisation.md`).

> **The headline lesson from the value sim:** the two highest value-per-minute movements in the whole program — loaded **Carries** and **DB Row** — were stranded on the always-skipped bonus Thursday (the *same* pathology v6.2 caught with the armor). v6.3 folds them onto the protected Tuesday, turning Push → **Upper**, so the aesthetic floor (Scott's top lever) no longer depends on a day that gets skipped.

## DESIGN INTENT

| Goal | How it's served | Priority when squeezed |
|------|-----------------|------------------------|
| Play better basketball at 38 | Game, skills, power/sprints, decel, conditioning, mobility | — |
| Longevity / body-comp insurance | Strength base, aerobic intervals, deficit, leanness | — |
| Look good | **Upper-body hypertrophy** (to failure) + leanness | earned |
| Lean, powerful, non-chafing legs | Heavy low-rep **at 2–3 RIR (not failure)** + power + conditioning; **no hypertrophy volume** | base |
| Injury-proof, stable body | Decel/landing, eccentric hamstring, ankle/calf/adductor prehab, athletic core | woven in |

**Lean-leg mechanism (corrected v6.1):** legs stay strong-but-small by training the *nervous system*, not the muscle — heavy, low reps (3–5), **2–3 reps in reserve, explosive concentric, never to failure.** Failure + volume = hypertrophy; that's reserved for the upper body. Chafing is also a leanness problem the deficit + conditioning attack.

## THE "EARN IT" MODEL (fractal) — v6.2 reframe

**The 4-day base IS the program. You succeed at 90%+ by hitting four days. The rest is a pure bonus you can never "fail."** The simulation showed the guilt of missing "earned" days every week is the #1 quit risk — so the bonus carries zero failure weight. Hit it when flush, ignore it when not, no scorekeeping.

**Week level — mnemonic: RUN · LOWER · UPPER · GAME = the week.**
- **THE PROGRAM (4 days):** Sun Run · Mon Lower · Tue **Upper** · Wed Game. Hitting these = a successful week, full stop.
- **ACCELERATOR (zero-guilt, only on genuine surplus):** Fri upper pump = **the aesthetic accelerator** (the weekly volume that builds the V-taper — your top lever) · Thu pull micro · Sat opportunistic shooting.
- **Best-value work moved off the skipped day (v6.3):** the two highest value-per-minute movements — **DB Row + a loaded Carry** — migrate off bonus Thursday onto **protected Tuesday Upper.** So the aesthetic floor happens even on a 4-day week instead of riding a day that gets skipped.
- **Armor on a protected day (v6.2, validated by v6.3):** the non-negotiable injury-prevention work (soleus/calf, adductor, anti-rotation) stays **folded into Mon Lower.** The combination sim scored its *offensive* value near-zero — which is exactly *why* it gets skipped — so it's protected as **high-priority insurance**, never its own block. Its value is preventing the missed weeks a calf/groin strain costs, which the value math can't see.

**Set level**
- **Upper (size goal):** 3 sets, last to failure, double progression (3×~6; hit 6 on last set → add load; climb until you can't hit 6).
- **Lower (lean/power goal):** 3 sets, 3–5 reps, **2–3 RIR, explosive — NOT to failure.** Progress load when all sets hit the top rep at target RIR.
- **RUSH:** 2 sets. **EMERGENCY (<12 min):** 1 set, top movements.

Rest is auto-regulated via sleep + flexing to energy. No fixed rest day; Sat is the first to drop.

## TEMPO

- **Upper:** lower 3–4 s · pause 1–2 s · lift 1–2 s with intent.
- **Lower (power bias):** lower 2–3 s controlled · brief pause · **lift as fast as possible** (compensatory acceleration — bar speed is the point).

## SUN @ KEILOR — RUNNING (while Audrey reps)

Freshest day relative to the game. He's at a running venue — consolidate all running here.
- **Warm-up:** 10 min easy + drills (A-skips, build-ups).
- **Speed:** acceleration sprints — 6–8 × 15–25 m, walk-back full recovery. *(Ramp: build-up strides 70–80% only in wk 1–2.)*
- **Aerobic:** intervals — Norwegian 4×4 (4 × [4 min @90–95% HRmax / 3 min @70%]) once recovered; **Zone-2 / 3×3 during the re-entry ramp.** Polar H10 → Garmin.

Do speed BEFORE the aerobic intervals (alactic when fresh, then the engine work).

## MON — LOWER (power + lean strength + prehab)

Order: prep → jumps (fresh) → strength → tissue/decel.

**Jumps (full recovery, quality):** pogos → box jumps (land soft, step down) → lateral/skater bounds. *(Approach/depth jumps gated — see ramp.)*

| # | Strength (lean lever) | Sets | Notes |
|---|----|------|-------|
| 1 | Bulgarian Split Squat | 3 × 3–5 ea @ 2–3 RIR | 2×21 kg DB. Explosive up. **Not to failure.** **Re-entry: 6–8 reps for the first ~month** — the deconditioned knee hates low-rep heavy (sim flagged this); drop to explosive 3–5 once pain-free. |
| 2 | Single-leg RDL | 3 × 4–5 ea @ 2–3 RIR | 24 kg KB. |
| 3 | Lateral Lunge / Cossack | 2 × 6–8 ea | **Frontal-plane** — was missing. |
| 4 | Hamstring slider / assisted Nordic | 2 × 5–6, **submax** | Eccentric — sprint-hamstring insurance. Not to failure during ramp. |

**THE ARMOR BLOCK (non-negotiable — this is why it's on a protected day).** Highest-ROI work in the program; skipping it is what caused the simulated calf strain. ~6–8 min, every Monday:
| Armor | Sets | Why |
|-------|------|-----|
| Bent-knee (soleus) Calf Raise | 2 × 10–12 | **Highest-ROI movement (25).** Absorbs the most jump/run load = calf/Achilles insurance. |
| Single-leg straight Calf Raise | 2 × 8 ea | Gastrocnemius. |
| Copenhagen (or regression) | 2 × 5–8 ea | Adductor = groin-strain insurance. |
| Pallof anti-rotation | 1–2 × 10 ea | Post-up trunk stability — migrated off the skipped Thursday. |

**Decel ladder (before any Sat shuttles):** snap-downs → stick landings → low box step-off-and-stick → 5 m accelerate-to-stop → lateral shuffle-to-stick → 45° cut @ 50/70/85%. Add reactive cuts only once controlled & pain-free.

## TUE — UPPER (push to failure + protected pull/carry — v6.3)

The value sim made this an **Upper** day, not push-only: it folds in the two highest value-per-minute movements in the program (DB Row, loaded Carry) so the aesthetic floor stops depending on the skipped Thursday. Push to failure (size goal); pull at RIR; carry as a short heavy finish. ~45–55 min.

**Push (to failure):** Incline DB Press 3×6 · Lateral Raise 3×15–20 · Skull Crusher→Reverse-Press flow (not standalone heavy skull crushers — sim flagged elbow tweaks). **DB OHP optional** (incline press already covers front delts; add only if shoulders are fresh).
**Pull (protected — the v6.3 add):** DB Row 3–4 hard sets · (Chin-up/Pull-up if a bar's handy — chin-up grip adds the arm/aesthetic the sim credited).
**Carry finish:** 4–8 total loaded carries (suitcase/offset rotation, not always max farmer) — grip + trunk + traps, stop before form degrades.

Legs rest before Wed game. **Guardrail:** keep total ~45–55 min and don't tax grip/feet — Wed's game needs them fresh.

> **Intensity rule (all 5 sims confirmed):** the first week you feel *great* is your highest-risk week — that's when you overreach and tweak a shoulder/elbow or strain a calf. When you feel good, add ONE notch (a little load or one set), not three. Earn the jump.

## THU — ACCELERATOR: PULL MICRO (zero-guilt, genuinely droppable)

**v6.3 made this truly optional.** The load-bearing pull (DB Row + Carry) moved to protected Tuesday, so Thursday is no longer carrying anything the week depends on — it's pure *extra* pull/shoulder-health volume for the aesthetics top-up. ~15 min, one decision:

**Pull-ups → Face pull (or rear delt) → one med-ball rotational throw set.** That's it. Loads only the upper body. Hammer curls only if you're flush.

- If you skip it: **zero guilt** — your week was a success at 4 days *and* the load-bearing pull already happened Tuesday. Core/adductor armor already happened Monday.
- Optional easy Zone-2 15–20 min if you want it.

## FRI — THE AESTHETIC ACCELERATOR (upper hypertrophy volume — reach for it first)

**v6.3 reframe.** The value sim's one blind spot (both reviewers flagged it): its coverage math prices the *2nd weekly hit* on a muscle at ≈0, but that repeated volume is exactly where V-taper/arm hypertrophy is built. Since **aesthetics is your top lever and the 4-day base serves it least**, this volume isn't "marginal bonus" — it's the session that moves the *look* fastest. Still zero-guilt for adherence, but of the three accelerator days, **reach for this one first.**

~35 min, realistic. 3×10–20, last to failure + optional drop:
Incline DB Press · Lateral Raise · DB Row or Pullover · Face Pull (or Rear Delt Fly) · Lying Supinated Curl + Skull-Crusher→Reverse-Press superset. *(Add Incline Fly only if sleep + soreness are green.)* Then shoot at the girls' Friday-night games.

At a hoop (girls' venue or carved time). Low grind.
- **Shooting:** form → free throws → range (locked ≥1×/wk).
- **Post-up:** drop step, up-and-under, pivots, finish through contact (Thursday's rotational core cashes out here).
- **Optional COD finisher:** a few controlled shuttles — **not** max suicides, and only after the Monday decel ladder is pain-free. Drop when recovery is poor.

Dribbling at home daily-ish (no hoop). Shooting at drop-off court + girls' game venues (Fri/Sat). Post-up footwork at home; finish at a hoop when available.

## RE-ENTRY RAMP — RSV (~1 mo) + post-fast immune hole — criteria-gated, not calendar

Both reviewers: post-infection return is governed by *symptom resolution + tolerance*, not the calendar; no failure early; sprints/plyo are the hamstring/Achilles trap if rushed.

**Recovery gate (before "normal" training):** maintenance calories — **no deficit** — until resting HR, sleep, appetite, respiratory symptoms, and session RPE are normal for 7 straight days. Stop immediately for chest pain, undue breathlessness, palpitations, or syncope.

| Phase | Lifting | Conditioning | Power/speed | Gate to advance |
|-------|---------|--------------|-------------|------------------|
| **Wk 1–2** | 3 sessions max, **RPE 5–6, no failure**, 2–3 RIR everywhere | Zone-2 / 3×3 only — no 4×4 | Build-up strides 70–80%, easy pogos, landing mechanics — **no max sprints / depth jumps** | Symptom-free + green recovery markers 7 days |
| **Wk 3** | Add 3rd set; failure on low-risk isolation only | One 4×4 **or** sprint prep — not both | True acceleration sprints; lateral bounds | No next-morning tendon/hamstring soreness |
| **Wk 4+** | Full engine (upper to failure, lower RIR) | Full 4×4 | Approach jumps; **depth jumps only after gate ↓** | **20 pain-free single-leg calf raises/side + controlled stick landings + no hamstring tightness after strides** |

## WHAT THE 4-DAY BASE ALONE DELIVERS (the realistic floor)

Even if Thu/Fri/Sat never happen, the base covers: legs (Mon strength+jumps + Sun running), push (Tue), the game (Wed conditioning + skills), the armor (Mon), and speed (Sun). The only things that live *only* in the bonus are extra pull volume and extra upper hypertrophy. So a 4-day week still trains everything that prevents injury and most of what drives the goals — the bonus just accelerates the look.

- Daily-ish microdose (no session needed): 5–8 min dribbling / post-up footwork at home.

Base: Legs 2× lean (Mon + Sun run) · Push 1× (Tue) · **Pull 1× (Tue — DB Row + Carry, v6.3)** · Armor/core 1× (Mon) · Speed 1× (Sun/Mon) · Aerobic 1× (Sun) + game · Game 1× (Wed). Accelerator adds: extra Pull (Thu) · Upper hypertrophy volume (Fri) · COD + shooting (Sat). **v6.3 net:** the base now trains pull, not just push — so a 4-day week covers the full upper body, not half.

## NUTRITION & SLEEP

- **Maintenance until immune-recovered** (recovery gate above). Then 400–500 kcal/day deficit, moderate.
- Protein ~200 g/day (2.0 g/kg).
- **The lean-legs goal lives or dies on the deficit, not the training.** Every sim that ran the deficit got lean legs / no chafing (Gemini 5/5); every sim where illness + stress sabotaged eating did not (Haiku 2/5). The RIR/explosive leg *method* works — the *outcome* is a nutrition problem. At realistic adherence, **budget 18–24 weeks for the leg/chafing result, not 12.**
- **Sleep: 7+ hrs is the target, not a 10 PM cage.** The sim flagged a hard 10 PM lights-out as a quit-risk — it's often the only time for your wife or a clear inbox. Protect sleep, but don't let the rule itself become a stressor. Sleep is still the real rest day.
