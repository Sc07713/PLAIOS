## PART A — Movement scoring index

### Methodology

Each movement scored 1–5 on three axes, then a composite:

- **Risk** — injury likelihood × severity for *this* athlete (38, post-illness, deconditioned). Higher = worse.
- **Reward** — adaptation value weighted to his goal stack: basketball power/skill, injury-prevention, upper aesthetics, lean legs, longevity. Higher = better.
- **Efficiency** — stimulus per minute including setup/logistics cost. Higher = better.
- **ROI = (Reward × Efficiency) ÷ Risk**, rounded. The weighting *is* the Reward axis (goal-aligned), divided by the injury cost. ROI ≥ 12 = protect/prioritise; ≤ 4 = scrutinise/gate.

### Index takeaways

1. **The highest-ROI work is cheap, safe prehab/skill** — soleus, Pallof, carries, med-ball, shooting, decel, Nordic, Copenhagen. Several of these were **stranded on the always-skipped Thursday/Saturday.** Objectively, the program was orphaning its best-value movements.
2. **Cut/gate candidates (low ROI / high risk):** depth jumps (gate hard), standalone heavy skull crusher (elbow → use the flow), DB OHP (redundant → optional).
3. **Bulgarian split squat** is high-reward but its risk score (deconditioned knee) argues for the gentler re-entry rep range.

## PART B — Virtual-Scott 12-week simulation (5 models, blind)

Five independent simulators — **Claude Opus 4.8, Sonnet 4.6, Haiku 4.5, OpenAI GPT-5.5 (Codex), Google Gemini** — each "lived" v6.1 for 12 weeks, blind to each other.

### Convergent findings (the binding signal — all or nearly all 5 agreed)

| Finding | Models | Detail |
|---------|:------:|--------|
| **BASE holds (~70–90%), EARNED collapses (~25–30%)** | 5/5 | Real ceiling = 4–5 sessions/wk, never 7. The earn-it floor *works* — the floor is what survives. |
| **Thursday Pull+Core is the dead slot** | 5/5 | Dropped first or near-first by everyone. Lowest dopamine + hated core + day-after-game + "earned" = no defensive priority. |
| **Protected spine = Wed game > Tue push > Mon lower** | 5/5 | Identity/social (game), visible payoff (push), training-identity (lower). Sun run fragile to weather/family. |
| **"Earned days = weekly guilt tax" is the #1 quit risk** | 4/5 (Opus, Sonnet, Codex, ~Gemini) | Missing 3 of 7 slots every week reads as chronic failure → identity erosion → quit. Worse than skipping rest. |
| **Calf/Achilles/ankle injury from skipped prep + overreach** | 5/5 | Recurring ~wk5–10 calf event, often the single biggest morale/physical setback. Gemini credited the prehab for *preventing* a Grade-2 ankle — when it was actually done. |
| **"Push too hard when feeling good" = injury vector** | 5/5 | The first great-feeling week → overreach → calf or shoulder/elbow tweak. Repeated across all. |
| **Upper-body look = most consistent WIN (3.5–4/5)** | 5/5 | Tue push was protected; visible lean hypertrophy. Capped because Fri (the volume driver) only landed ~half the weeks. |
| **Lean legs SPLIT (2–5/5) — hinges on the deficit running** | 5/5 | Mechanism (RIR/explosive) endorsed as working; *outcome* depended on diet, which illness+stress sabotaged. Gemini 5/5 / Haiku 2/5. |
| **Conditioning middling (2.5–4/5)** | 5/5 | 4×4 intervals rarely done properly; calf interruptions; but game fitness improved. |

### Convergent #1 adherence fix (all 5, phrased differently)

> **Make the 4-day base THE program (success at 90%+); reframe Thu/Fri/Sat as pure zero-guilt bonus; shrink the earned work to micro-doses (a single 15–20 min named anchor, not "Pull+Core"); and fold the non-negotiable calf/soleus + adductor prehab INTO a protected day (Mon) so the highest-ROI, injury-preventing work actually happens.**

Other convergent tweaks: gentler re-entry leg loads (Bulgarian 6–8 reps first ~month before explosive 3–5 — Sonnet/Haiku); decouple Fri/Sat from kids'-game logistics (Haiku); soften the 10 PM sleep mandate — it competes with the only couple/inbox time and reads as a cage (Gemini).

### The two lenses agree

The index says the best-value movements (soleus, Pallof, carries, decel, Nordic, shooting) are cheap, safe, and were **stranded on the skipped days.** The simulation says those exact days get skipped and that's where the **calf injury** comes from. Both point to one move: **migrate the high-ROI safe prehab onto the protected days; let the rest be true bonus.** → v6.2.

## PART C — Goal-weighted scorecard (the decision tool)

The lens Scott actually wanted: score each movement against his three explicit goals and prioritise by cross-domain value. **Aesthetics = his aesthetic** (shoulder/back width, lean waist, arms; leg *size* is NOT wanted, so legs score on lean/defined, not mass).

**Scoring rubric (0–5 per goal):**
- **Longevity (L):** VO2/cardio (mortality #1), muscle/strength (sarcopenia, metabolic), tissue resilience.
- **Aesthetics (A):** V-taper (shoulder/back width), leanness/low body fat (fixes "feeling fat" + chafe), upper chest, arms.
- **Basketball (B):** power/jump, speed, change-of-direction/decel, skill, game conditioning, contact/post-up stability.

**Weighting tested:** initially 40 L / 35 A / 25 B; **revised to equal 33/33/33 (2026-05-31)** so the score = (L+A+B)/3.

### Findings (robust to weighting)

1. **The compound LPP backbone + conditioning own the top under either weighting** — they double-dip across all three goals. Scott's "simple LPP is best" instinct is mathematically correct; functional complexity must earn its place.
2. **Conditioning (4×4) is the highest-value single item** — #1 longevity lever AND the leanness driver behind the aesthetics/chafe goal.
3. **Equal weighting lifts the athletic satellites** (jumps, decel, sprints, skills, single-leg) and eases the pure-aesthetic isolations (laterals, curls). Spread tightens; backbone unchanged.
4. **Three satellite classes, sized to goal:** pure-aesthetics (laterals/curls/abs — cheap V-taper layer); pure-basketball (skills score lowest on the math but are the *point* of the 25–33% B goal — keep on court/bonus time); injury-prehab (soleus/Copenhagen/Pallof — a risk multiplier protecting all three, justified as insurance not offense).
5. **Testosterone:** the top-of-board profile (compound multi-joint, posterior-chain/leg involvement, adequate-not-excess volume) + lifestyle levers (sleep, ~10–15% body fat, no over-fasting/calorie-crashing) already pulls T the right way. Measuring = blood test + nutrition/sleep layer.
