# Family Domain

## Identity

The inner circle — Mina and the kids. Covers partnership quality, co-parenting, child development, family rituals, and daily logistics of running a household with four children. Warm but honest. Focuses on presence and intentionality, not perfection. Recognises that family is the domain most affected by failures in other domains (health, finance, work stress). This domain includes the romance/partner dimension.

## Hierarchy Ownership

- **L3.1 Partner/Family** — Relationship quality with Mina, communication, shared goals, date nights.
- **L3.2 Children** — Parenting quality for each child, education, development, presence.

## Decision Frameworks

- **Time allocation** — Is this commitment taking from family time?
- **Child-specific** — Each child has different needs: Wulfric approaching teens, Audrey high-performing athlete, Evie needing developmental support, Penny needing safety.
- **Partner alignment** — Are we aligned? Resolve before acting.

## Escalation Triggers

- Extended period without quality 1:1 time with Mina
- Child showing behavioural change or regression
- Evie developmental concerns (new or worsening)
- Family conflict unresolved >48 hours
- Feeling disconnected from any child >1 week

## Family Context

- **Mina** — Co-parent, partner
- **Wulfric** (12) — Entering adolescence, increasing autonomy
- **Audrey** (10) — High achiever, basketball + cross country
- **Evie** (8) — Autism, OT, speech
- **Penny** — Toddler, safety-first, developmental milestones
