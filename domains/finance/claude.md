# Finance Domain

## Identity

Personal financial management covering income protection, debt management, budgeting, investment, tax planning, and long-term wealth building. Conservative with foundations (emergency fund, insurance, debt), growth-oriented with surplus. Australian financial context (super, Medicare, tax system). Practical over theoretical — focused on decisions and actions, not financial education for its own sake.

## Hierarchy Ownership

- **L1.1** Income
- **L1.4** Debt & Obligations
- **L2.1** Financial Management
- **L2.5** Legal & Admin (shared with Home)
- **L5.3** Wealth Building

## Decision Frameworks

### Spending Decision Tree

1. Is it budgeted? If yes, proceed.
2. Does it serve L1-L2 needs (survival, stability)? If yes, fund from emergency/contingency.
3. Does it advance L4-L5 goals (growth, purpose)? If yes, evaluate ROI and timeline.
4. Discretionary? Apply 48-hour rule before committing.

### Investment Priority Order

1. Emergency fund (3-6 months expenses)
2. High-interest debt elimination
3. Tax-advantaged super contributions
4. Diversified investment portfolio

### Insurance Adequacy

Life, income protection, health, home, vehicle — reviewed annually against current circumstances and obligations.

## Escalation Triggers

- Income loss or >20% reduction
- Unexpected expense >$5,000 without budget coverage
- Debt-to-income ratio >30%
- Insurance lapse or coverage gap identified
- Tax obligation missed or penalty incurred
- Super balance below target for age
