# Risk, Protection & Wealth Building

Insurance is the least exciting part of financial planning and the most catastrophic when it's wrong. Savings without direction becomes a number without meaning. This file covers both.

---

## Insurance Audit

| Cover Type | Purpose | Check |
|---|---|---|
| **Life insurance** | Replaces income for surviving partner + children | Adequate to clear all debt + fund living costs for X years? |
| **TPD** | Lump sum if permanently unable to work | Adequate to clear debt + fund modified lifestyle? |
| **Income protection** | Replaces income during temporary disability | Waiting period appropriate? Benefit period to 65 or shorter? Covers business owner income structure? |
| **Trauma/Critical illness** | Lump sum on diagnosis of specified conditions | Optional but valuable — covers gap between healthy and TPD |
| **Health insurance** | Private hospital + extras | Appropriate level? Excess set correctly for cash flow? |
| **Home & contents** | Property and possessions | Sum insured current? Not underinsured? |

**Key question for business owners:** If the primary earner is incapacitated for 6 months, what happens to the business *and* the household simultaneously? Most families insure for one scenario, not both.

---

## Estate Planning

- Wills current and reflecting actual wishes?
- Powers of attorney (financial + medical) in place for both partners?
- Binding death benefit nominations on super current?
- Business succession addressed? (What happens to the business if the founder dies or is permanently disabled?)
- Guardianship for children nominated?

**Review trigger:** Any major life event (birth, property purchase, business structure change, relationship change) or every 3 years minimum.

## Goal Framework

| Timeframe | Category | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Short-term (0-2 years)** | Immediate needs | Emergency fund, holiday, home repair, debt payoff |
| **Medium-term (2-7 years)** | Life transitions | Property purchase/upgrade, school fees, vehicle replacement, business exit preparation |
| **Long-term (7+ years)** | Wealth and legacy | Retirement, children's university, investment portfolio, intergenerational wealth |

Each goal needs: a target amount, a deadline, a funding source (which account/investment), and a progress tracking method. Vague goals ("save more") produce vague results.

---

## Investment Principles

General principles (not specific investment advice):

- Emergency fund in cash/offset — accessible within 24 hours, not invested in volatile assets
- Time horizon determines asset allocation — longer horizon tolerates more growth assets (shares, property), shorter horizon requires more defensive assets (cash, fixed interest)
- Diversification across asset classes, geographies, and structures
- Fees compound just like returns — low-cost index options deserve consideration alongside actively managed funds
- Business owners are already heavily concentrated in a single asset (their business). Personal investments should diversify *away* from the business, not double down

---

## Business Exit & Liquidity Event Planning

For business owners actively planning an exit:

- What is the realistic post-tax proceeds estimate? (Conservative, not aspirational.)
- Where will the proceeds go? (Debt clearance, investment, super, property, lifestyle — in what proportions?)
- What is the household income plan post-exit? (Investment income, employment, semi-retirement, new venture?)
- What professional team is needed? (M&A advisor, tax accountant, financial planner, lawyer.)
- What is the household's minimum annual income requirement post-exit? Does the expected investment portfolio support this sustainably (typically 3.5-4% withdrawal rate)?

This planning should begin 2-3 years before a target exit, not after the deal closes.
