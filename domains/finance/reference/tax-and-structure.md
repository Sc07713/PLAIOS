# Tax & Structure Optimisation

The gap between gross income and after-tax household cash flow can be enormous — and is often the most accessible lever for increasing savings without reducing lifestyle. This layer is especially critical for business owners.

---

## Business Owner Extraction Strategy

How income moves from the business to the household matters significantly:

- **Salary/wages:** Simple, predictable, generates super guarantee entitlements. But fully taxed at marginal rate.
- **Dividends:** Tax-effective if franking credits available. But doesn't generate super.
- **Trust distributions:** Flexible — can split income to lower-tax partner. But requires appropriate structure and compliance.
- **Combination:** Most business owners benefit from a blended approach optimised annually with their accountant.

**The advisor's role:** Flag when the current extraction method appears suboptimal and recommend a review with the accountant. This is not tax advice — it's pattern recognition.

---

## Superannuation Optimisation

Super is the most tax-advantaged savings vehicle available in Australia. Common missed opportunities:

- Concessional contributions up to the cap ($30,000/year as of 2024-25) — particularly for business owners who often underpay themselves super
- Spouse contributions for non-working or lower-income partners
- Carry-forward unused concessional cap from prior years (up to 5 years)
- Salary sacrifice arrangements for the employed partner
- Reviewing super fund performance, fees, and insurance within super

---

## Deduction & Offset Awareness

Maintain a running list of potential deductions and offsets. Common categories for business owner households:

- Home office expenses
- Professional development
- Income protection insurance premiums
- Investment property costs
- Charitable donations
- Private health insurance rebate review

**Annual rhythm:** Pre-EOFY review (May-June) to identify and action opportunities before the year closes. Post-EOFY debrief with accountant to capture lessons for next year.
